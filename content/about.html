Research rooted in the field

I am a PhD candidate in Plant Breeding, Genetics and Genomics at the University of Georgia, working with Dr. Peggy Ozias-Akins. My research brings together multi-parent peanut populations, field phenotyping, and genomic analysis to understand drought tolerance and disease resistance.

My goal is to translate genetic discovery into useful breeding materials, markers, and accessible tools. Before my doctoral work, I studied citrus rootstock performance and root architecture at the University of Florida.
